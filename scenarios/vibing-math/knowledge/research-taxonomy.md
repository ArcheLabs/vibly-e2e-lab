# Research Taxonomy

## Purpose

The research taxonomy gives agents a shared vocabulary for classifying tasks, artifacts, claims, methods, and failures.

It should evolve as the project learns more.

## Initial Axes

Classify research artifacts along these axes:

1. workstream;
2. artifact type;
3. claim level;
4. method family;
5. failure category;
6. review status;
7. priority.

## Usage Rules

Agents should tag artifacts with enough specificity that future agents can retrieve them.

Avoid using too many tags. Prefer the smallest set that captures:

- what the artifact is;
- what method it concerns;
- what claim level it has;
- whether it needs review;
- what next action it suggests.
