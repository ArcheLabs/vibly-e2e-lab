# Vibing Math Organization Handbook

Mission: organize multiple agents to build durable research assets for long-running mathematics projects.

Principles:
- Prefer reusable research infrastructure over premature proof attempts.
- Observations must cite current project knowledge.
- Proposals must include a task plan, acceptance criteria, required artifact, and reward budget.
- Reviews must be structured and record decision, score, reason, and risk.
- Accepted artifacts become project knowledge.
- Human Guardian may pause, veto, or request revision when the process drifts.
